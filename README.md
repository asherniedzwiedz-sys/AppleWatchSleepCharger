# Apple Watch Sleep Charger

Is there a charger you can wear on an Apple Watch overnight, so it charges while it tracks your sleep, the way the Whoop battery pack does?

**Short answer: no. No such product exists for Apple Watch, and the hardware design makes one unlikely.**

*Researched November 2026.*

## Why the Whoop approach doesn't carry over

- **Whoop:** the battery pack clips onto the top of the sensor module, so the sensor stays against your skin while it charges.
- **Apple Watch:** it charges through a magnetic puck on the back, which is also where the heart-rate sensors and wrist detection sit. Any charger on the watch covers those sensors. You lose heart-rate data, and the watch will probably decide it isn't on your wrist. Either way, sleep tracking breaks.

That's a physical limit, not a missing accessory.

## The one product that tried: Reserve Strap

- A band with a battery that charged the watch through a hidden 6-pin diagnostic port on early Apple Watches.
- Apple turned off that port in watchOS 2.0.1 and later, and in April 2016 the company stopped shipping. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2016/04/18/reserve-strap-suspends-shipments/), [Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/apple-watch/apple-update-kills-reserve-strap-charging-band-dead-2607))
- *Not verified:* Apple is reported to have removed the diagnostic port entirely starting with Series 7, which would close that route for good.

**About "wearable Apple Watch charger" listings:** these are portable puck power banks. If you use one on your wrist, it still covers the sensors.

## What works: a short charge before bed

Apple's figures, from the [Apple Watch battery page](https://www.apple.com/watch/battery/), checked November 2026:

| Model | Charge for sleep | Fast charge | Battery life (normal / Low Power Mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Watch Ultra 4 | 5 min → up to 10 h of sleep tracking | ~45 min to 80% | up to 50 h / up to 84 h |
| Apple Watch Series 12 | 5 min → up to 10 h of sleep tracking | ~30 min to 80% | up to 24 h / up to 38 h |
| Apple Watch SE 3 | 8 min → up to 8 h of sleep tracking | ~45 min to 80% | up to 18 h / up to 32 h |

Apple measured these with its Magnetic Fast Charger to USB-C Cable, so expect slower results with other chargers.

### Routine

1. Put the watch on the charger while you shower or brush your teeth before bed. A few minutes covers the night.
2. Turn on the sleep charging reminder, which nudges you before bedtime if the battery is low. *Not verified:* it's probably under Settings → Sleep on the watch, but it may have moved in the current watchOS.
3. **Ultra 4 owners:** at up to 50 hours, you can charge at any point during the day and still have enough for the night.

## Bottom line

If you want 24/7 wear with no charging routine at all, that's what Whoop is built for. Apple Watch isn't, and no accessory changes that today. The practical fix is a 5–10 minute top-up before bed.
